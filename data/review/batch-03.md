*Family Mexican restaurant · West End, Alameda · Deep cut · morning, midday, evening*

## Shuei-Do Manju Shop — Gem 83
*Handmade manju shop, since 1953 · Japantown, San Jose · Deep cut · morning, midday*

**Story.** Hatsuyo and John Ozawa founded this Japantown manju shop in 1953. With no children to take it over, John recruited his fishing buddy's daughter and her husband, Judy and Tom Kumamaru, who bought it in 1988 without seeing the kitchen or a recipe. The Ozawas stayed five months to teach them, and everything is still made by hand.

**Order this.** A box of mixed manju; the kinako, dusted in soybean powder over white-bean filling, is the one to try.

**Why this score.** The Bay Area's oldest traditional Japanese confectioner, passed between two families on a handshake. A small sweet shop, so the room is modest, but the story and craft are rare.  
Uniqueness 90 · Atmosphere 66 · Story 90 · Signature item 86
