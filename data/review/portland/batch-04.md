## Kay's Bar — Gem 61
*1934 corner tavern · Sellwood-Moreland, Portland · Open secret · midday, evening, late*

**Story.** Kay's dates to 1934, the year after Prohibition ended, and sits on the corner of Milwaukie and Bybee. Willamette Week judged it too polished to count as a dive and passed along rumors of a ghost. Its cocktails have drawn more praise than a neighborhood tavern strictly needs.

**Order this.** A cocktail, ideally at the late-night happy hour, which starts at 10 pm Sunday to Wednesday and 11 pm Thursday to Saturday.

## El Sombrero Tapatio — Gem 57
*Family Mexican restaurant · Parkrose, Portland · Open secret · midday*

**Story.** Elvira Navarro came from Jalisco in the 1970s and worked her way up from busser to server to cook, the last ten years in this Parkrose kitchen. Relatives bought the restaurant, name and all, from a retiring owner, and it turns 25 in 2027. The mole and chile verde are recipes she brought from Mexico.

**Order this.** The mole or the chile verde, both Elvira's family recipes, with a side of the refried beans. The birria began as a special and stayed on the menu because people kept asking for it.

**Why this score.** A good family Mexican spot rather than a one-of-a-kind, lifted by a real story: a cook who worked her way up and recipes she brought from Mexico. We have nothing on the room and no hours, and every story fact comes from one profile.
