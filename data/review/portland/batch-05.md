## Original Hotcake House — Gem 57
*Counter-service hotcake diner · Brooklyn, Portland · Local legend · morning, midday, evening*

**Story.** The Original Hotcake House has held the foot of the Ross Island Bridge since 1963, a counter-service greasy spoon that spent decades feeding Portland's after-bar crowd. It no longer stays open around the clock, but the feel survived, and the hotcakes still come with big scoops of butter sliding into the syrup.

**Order this.** The hotcakes, obviously. For a little of everything, get the combo of two eggs and two cakes.

**Why this score.** A real 1963 institution with a hotcake people know by name, but the all-night hours that made it famous are gone and our sources say little about the room itself.

## John Street Cafe — Gem 48
*Breakfast and lunch cafe · St. Johns, Portland · Open secret · morning, midday*

**Story.** A breakfast-and-lunch cafe on the St. Johns stretch of Lombard that Willamette Week was already recommending in 2011. Omelets anchor the menu, the filbert-and-currant pancakes come huge, and there's a sunny patio. It changed hands at some point, and one restaurant guide says the new owners kept things as they were.

## B&b Pizza Co. — Gem 56
*Old-school family pizza parlor · Troutdale, Troutdale · Deep cut · midday, evening*

**Story.** Co-owner Jason Blume spent nearly 20 years behind the bar and in the owner's seat at Skyland Pub before opening B&b in fall 2024. He wanted the feel of the family pizza parlors he grew up with, salad bar included. The thin crust is house sourdough with cornmeal, and the sauce is made in-house.

**Order this.** A thin sourdough-crust pie and a trip to the salad bar. For a quick lunch, slices come with up to three toppings of your choice.

**Why this score.** A modest draft: a two-year-old family pizza parlor whose old-school concept, salad bar and sourdough-cornmeal crust set it apart from chain pizza, and local readers voted it best pizza in 2026. The story is short, the room is a big family space with TVs, and only one outlet has covered it.

## Nicholas Restaurant — Gem 52
*Lebanese family restaurant · Buckman, Portland · Local legend · midday, evening*

**Story.** Nicholas Dibe arrived from Lebanon, then at war, and opened this family restaurant on SE Grand in 1987. After 33 years there, the family moved it to a roomier building on SE Madison in 2021 and turned the old space into a fast-casual spinoff, Beirut Bites.

**Order this.** Go with friends and share a spread of Lebanese plates at the place Willamette Week readers voted Portland's best Mediterranean restaurant in 2026.

**Why this score.** A strong family story since 1987 and a 2026 readers' poll win carry it. The 2021 move to a larger, more polished building costs it atmosphere, and we could not source a specific dish, which holds down signature.
