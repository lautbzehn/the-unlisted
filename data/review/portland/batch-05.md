## Jam on Hawthorne — Gem 42
*House-jam breakfast cafe · Buckman, Portland · Local legend · morning, midday*

**Story.** Jam on Hawthorne takes its name from the jams it cooks in house, and it serves breakfast every day in a big, busy room. It grew into a former bar space next door in 2011. Vegans eat well here, with tofu scramble and tempeh bacon, and readers keep ranking it among Portland's top brunches.

**Order this.** The two-egg breakfast with a mimosa, the pairing Willamette Week once called the move here, or the tofu scramble with tempeh bacon if you're vegan. Expect a wait.

**Why this score.** A well-liked, busy brunch cafe with one real quirk, the jam made on site, and good vegan plates. Uniqueness and story are modest: it is a solid version of a common thing, and we found little history beyond the owners and a 2011 expansion.
