## Lutz Tavern — Gem 67
*Old neighborhood tavern · Woodstock, Portland · Open secret · midday, evening, late*

**Story.** The Lutz family opened it in 1947, and the Barisich family ran it from 1954 until it closed in 2010. New owners reopened it in 2011 with liquor, Northwest taps and a proper kitchen. In the late 1990s its dollar cans of Pabst helped make the beer cool again, though EJ's disputes the credit.

**Order this.** A burger from the kitchen added in 2011, and the pickles, which took top honors in a local pickle contest in 2013.

**Why this score.** Few Portland bars have a story this long or this odd, from two family eras to a disputed claim on the Pabst revival. The food is ordinary tavern fare, so signature stays low, and recent coverage is thin.

## Taqueria Rico Taco — Gem 52
*Late-night Rockwood taqueria · Rockwood, Gresham · Deep cut · morning, midday, evening, late*

**Story.** Taqueria Rico Taco has been feeding Gresham from SE 181st since 1999, with handmade tacos at the center of the menu. When SNAP benefits stopped during the November 2025 federal shutdown, it gave two tacos and a fountain drink to anyone who showed a SNAP card at the counter.

**Order this.** The handmade tacos, the thing it has built its name on. It opens at 8 am and stays open until 11:30 pm most nights, so it covers breakfast through a late dinner.

**Why this score.** A modest story: 26 years on one corner, a kind gesture during the 2025 shutdown, and hours that run past midnight on weekends. It is one taqueria among many, I found nothing on the room, and only one press piece.

## Helvetia Tavern — Gem 60
*Country burger tavern · Helvetia, Hillsboro · Local legend · midday, evening*

**Story.** Helvetia Tavern dates itself to 1946, and in 2025 Chowhound named it Oregon's best hole-in-the-wall burger. Rows of trucker hats cover the ceiling, a nod to the working crowd, and the burgers come with the house goop, a sauce close to Russian dressing.

**Order this.** A burger with goop, the Jumbo if you're hungry, and fries.

**Why this score.** The hat-covered ceiling and the goop-dressed burger give it a real identity, and Chowhound's 2025 nod backs up the burger. The history is muddled, with founding dates from 1922 to 1946 depending on the source, and current hours are unconfirmed.
