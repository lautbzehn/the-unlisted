## Dumpling Inn & Shanghai Saloon — Gem 63
*Convoy dumpling house · Kearny Mesa, San Diego · Open secret · midday, evening*

**Story.** Dumpling Inn started in 1994 as an eight-table storefront on Convoy making dumplings, potstickers and xiao long bao by hand, among the first in San Diego to serve soup dumplings. In 2015 it moved next door into a former Korean market, adding an Old Shanghai-themed saloon. The Vuong family bought it in the early 2020s.

**Why this score.** One of the places that taught San Diego about xiao long bao. It has grown into a 230-seat room with a bar, so it is no longer small.

## Cafe La Maze — Gem 77
*1940 Hollywood stopover steakhouse · National City, National City · Open secret · midday, evening*

**Story.** Marcel Lamaze, who ran a Sunset Strip cafe for the movie crowd, built this one in 1940 as a stopover for Hollywood on the way to Tijuana's Caliente racetrack, with a hidden gambling room upstairs. Stars came to the opening, and photos of Dietrich, Gable and Flynn still line the walls. Police raided the gambling in 1947.

**Order this.** Prime rib.

**Why this score.** One of the best backstories in the county, in a room that still looks the part. It is a full-service steakhouse, so it is less of a counter find.
