## Rudford's Restaurant — Gem 63
*24-hour diner since 1949 · North Park, San Diego · Local legend · morning, midday, evening, late*

**Story.** Tommy Rudford bought the place on El Cajon Boulevard in 1949 and built it on scratch cooking. His daughters ran it after he died in 1972, until Leon Gilman bought it in 1983. It has stayed open 24 hours a day, every day, ever since, serving breakfast at any hour to utility crews, families and night owls.

**Order this.** Breakfast, whatever time it is.

**Why this score.** A diner that has not closed its doors since 1949. The food is honest home-style comfort, not the reason to go; the round-the-clock room is.  
Uniqueness 72 · Atmosphere 78 · Story 80 · Signature item 54 · Under the radar 25

**Hours.** Open 24 hours, every day (diner site)

## Aqui Es Texcoco — Gem 72
*Cross-border lamb barbacoa · Chula Vista, Chula Vista · Open secret · morning, midday, evening*

**Story.** Francisco Perez's uncle started Aqui es Texcoco in Tijuana in 1990 and later sold it to Perez's parents. Perez worked weekends there while studying engineering, then opened this Chula Vista branch in 2008 after noticing how many Americans drove to Tijuana for barbacoa. The lamb is slow-cooked for hours in maguey leaves.

## Point Loma Seafoods — Gem 66
